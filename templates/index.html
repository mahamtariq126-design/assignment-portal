Submission guidelines

  1. 1
    Use your official name

    Exactly as it appears on your student ID card.

  2. 2
    Check your registration number

    Format such as FA22-BSE-001. Submissions without it are rejected.

  3. 3
    Share a working link

    Only http:// or https:// links are accepted. Make sure your instructor can open it.

  4. 4
    Keep your receipt

    Every submission is time-stamped below as proof of submission.
